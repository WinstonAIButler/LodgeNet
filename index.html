What's in the box

Four tools the Secretary and Treasurer will actually use

Everything is built around how a lodge already runs: officers, degrees, Stated and Special Communications, and the charities you raise money for.

Website

A lodge site in minutes

Pick your name, number and colors. Meeting schedule, officers, history and a "Become a Mason" inquiry form are filled in for you.

Merch store

Sell with purpose

Lodge shirts, pins, caps and 100-year anniversary coins. Choose which charity each sale supports and see proceeds per fundraiser.

Calendar

Meetings and fundraisers

Recurring Stated Communications, degree nights, and public fundraisers with goals, RSVPs and progress the community can follow.

Minutes

The Secretary's desk

Start from a business-meeting template, mark drafts for approval at the next Stated, and keep an archive members can search.
