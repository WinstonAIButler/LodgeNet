Features

Everything a lodge runs on, in one place

Four tools built around how a lodge already works, so the Secretary and Treasurer spend their evenings in fellowship instead of spreadsheets.

Website

A lodge site in minutes

Pick your lodge name, number, jurisdiction and colors. LodgeNet fills in the pages every lodge needs, and keeps them current from your calendar and officer list.

  • Home, Calendar, Store, Our History and Become a Mason pages, ready on day one
  • Meeting schedule and upcoming events pulled straight from your calendar
  • Officer roster that rolls over each year at installation
  • A "Become a Mason" inquiry form that goes to the Secretary
  • Connect the lodge Gmail so inquiries, RSVPs and receipts come from the lodge's own address
  • Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube and TikTok links on every page
  • Members-only area for dues, minutes and lodge notices
  • Free lodgenet.org address, or bring your own domain

Merch store

Sell with purpose

Lodge shirts, pins, caps, mugs and anniversary coins, printed on demand so nobody stores boxes in the lodge basement. Every sale is tied to the charity it supports.

  • Get paid straight into the lodge's PayPal, Venmo or Cash App
  • Choose which fundraiser or fund each sale supports
  • See exactly how much each item sends to charity
  • Print-on-demand fulfillment, so there's no inventory to buy
  • Limited runs for centennials, degree classes and installations
  • Sales reports the Treasurer can read at the Stated
Try it in the demo
Calendar

Meetings and fundraisers

One calendar for the brethren and the public. Lodge meetings stay inside the lodge; fundraisers go out to the whole town with goals, RSVPs and progress.

  • Recurring Stated Communications (for example, 2nd Tuesday at 7:30 PM)
  • Special Communications and degree nights for Master Masons
  • Public fundraisers with a goal, a progress bar and RSVPs
  • Online donations by PayPal, Venmo or Cash App, plus cash logged at the door
  • District calendar for visitations across lodges
Try it in the demo
Minutes

The Secretary's desk

Start from a business-meeting template, edit the draft, and mark it approved at the next Stated. Approved minutes are locked and archived for members to search.

  • Templates for Stated and Special Communications
  • Draft, awaiting approval and approved states
  • Approved minutes lock and can't be edited
  • Searchable archive in the members-only area
  • Records lodge business only. LodgeNet never stores ritual or degree work
