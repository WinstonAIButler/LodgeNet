Lodge Level

Everything a Blue Lodge needs online, published and taking payments.

$19per month
Everything in Lodge Starter, plus
  • Publish on a lodgenet.org address or your own domain
  • Print-on-demand store: shirts, pins, caps and coins
  • Fundraiser goals, RSVPs and donations
  • Payments straight to the lodge's PayPal, Venmo or Cash App
  • Unlimited officer logins and a members area

Paid levels bill monthly by card through Stripe, and you can cancel from your account at any time. Have a promo code? Enter it on Stripe's checkout page.
