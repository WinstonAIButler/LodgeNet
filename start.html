Step 1 · Create your account

Use your own email. You'll confirm it, and then come straight back here to pick your plan and set up your lodge.

Create account I already have one

What happens next

Step 2, choose Lodge Starter (free) or the Lodge plan ($19 a month). Step 3, enter your lodge's name and number. On the Lodge plan you then pay by card through Stripe, and the subscription renews monthly.

Step 2 · Choose your plan

Lodge Starter

$0 / month
  • Build your lodge website (preview only; publishing comes with the Lodge plan)
  • Lodge calendar with Stated Communications filled in for you
  • Members roll and officer chairs
  • Meeting minutes: short form, full write-up and PDF
  • 2 officer logins

Lodge

$19 / month
  • Everything in Lodge Starter
  • Publish your lodge website, on a lodgenet.org address or your own domain
  • Merch store: shirts, pins, caps and challenge coins, print-on-demand
  • Fundraiser goals, RSVPs and donations
  • Payments go straight to the lodge's PayPal, Venmo or Cash App
  • Unlimited officer logins and a members area
  • Billed monthly by card through Stripe. Cancel any time.

Have a promo code? Enter it on Stripe's checkout page. A full-discount code needs no card.
